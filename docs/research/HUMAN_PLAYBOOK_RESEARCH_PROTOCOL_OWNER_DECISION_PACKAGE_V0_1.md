## 2. Decision 1 — research governance

### Project-brain recommendation: APPROVE

Freeze the following principles:

- `SOURCE CLAIM != AI/PROJECT INTERPRETATION != MACHINE OPERATIONALIZATION`;
- claim labels `EXPLICIT / INFERRED / UNKNOWN`;
- exactly one source-justified `PRIMARY_BRANCH` before any backtest;
- if the source cannot justify one primary interpretation without seeing outcomes -> stop as ambiguous, do not mine alternatives for a winner;
- alternative operationalizations are explicit search freedom and cannot rescue a failed primary as “validation of the human claim”;
- branches/scope/parameters/material AI specification are locked before testing;
- lineage/mechanism identity and exposure survive renaming;
- source examples can themselves expose protected market dates;
- effective content/version date is the objective first exposure bound;
- missing observables cannot be replaced by a K-line proxy unless the substitute is justified before outcome testing and labeled `INFERRED`;
- negative/near-miss/no-trade evidence is first-class;
- family metric shopping is prohibited by one common acceptance contract;
- six products are not assumed to be six independent samples;
- evidence can end honestly as `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`;
- validation is an exam, with only a narrow objectively provable implementation-bug exception;
- final exposure is irreversible; a void implementation defect never restores unseen status;
- final-inspired descendants must wait for genuinely new unseen evidence.

### Strongest downside

This deliberately sacrifices some research throughput. A number of human ideas will remain ambiguous or insufficiently evidenced rather than being “made testable” by trying many definitions.

### Why project brain still recommends it

That sacrifice is the main defense preventing Human Playbook First from becoming disguised indicator/parameter mining.

## 3. Decision 2 — initial chronology

Canonical corpus: 2024-01-01 .. 2025-12-31.

### Project-brain recommendation: 12 / 6 / 6

```text
DEVELOPMENT     2024-01-01 .. 2024-12-31
VALIDATION      2025-01-01 .. 2025-06-30
FINAL_UNSEEN    2025-07-01 .. 2025-12-31
```

Why:
- simplest and easiest to audit;
- one full calendar year for source interpretation/debugging/development;
- clean equal validation/final reserves;
- simplest source-example exposure accounting;
- avoids rolling/repeated validation looks.

What it does NOT solve:
- low-frequency playbooks may not have enough events;
- correlated products reduce effective evidence;
- six-month windows may represent only a narrow market regime.

Those problems are handled by `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, not by changing the dates after results are visible.

### Alternative: 10 / 8 / 6

Trade two development months for two more validation months. This is defensible but less simple, and still leaves the same six-month final reserve.

### Not recommended initially: rolling validation

Too many repeated looks for a 24-month corpus; increases tuning freedom without creating enough genuinely independent blocks.

## 4. Decision 3 — evidence insufficiency

### Project-brain recommendation: APPROVE

Freeze `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` as a legitimate outcome distinct from PASS/FAIL.

Meaning:
- if pre-registered evidence sufficiency is not met, do not loosen thresholds;
- do not move split boundaries after seeing results;
- do not add products/timeframes/interpretations merely to obtain significance;
- preserve the candidate and wait for genuinely new evidence if appropriate.

This is especially important because the accepted corpus is only 24 months.

## 5. Common acceptance contract after approval

Every family must answer the same four questions:

1. positive net economic edge after pre-registered costs/slippage, with uncertainty treated explicitly;
2. evidence sufficiency under one project-wide, pre-registered statistical profile;
3. required risk/failure constraints pass;
4. chronology/exposure/search integrity remains valid.

Family-specific diagnostics may be added but may not substitute a convenient metric for the common contract.

The exact deterministic statistical implementation (confidence/precision method, dependence handling and event sufficiency calculation) will be a single versioned project-wide implementation profile, not a family-selectable menu. It must be completed before the first validation window is opened. Changing it after validation exposure creates a new research-protocol version and requires the normal semantic-change gate.
