
They are not promoted merely because interviews or rankings exist. The project should prefer candidates with:

- repeated or longitudinal evidence;
- clear observation surface;
- lower dependence on pure winner storytelling.

## 6. Source-quality rule for this lane

Do not collapse:

```text
competition rank
-> interview popularity
-> trader authority
-> playbook validity
```

Correct route:

```text
competition/public record
-> discovery
-> identity/provenance check
-> original long-form material
-> Source Dossier
-> later protocol-governed claim extraction
```

If the original article cannot be pinned, preserve the derivative provenance chain explicitly rather than upgrading it to `TRACEABLE_ORIGINAL`.
